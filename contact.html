Follow the Road

Life Quest is a real-life RPG app for returning to real life. It is currently in development, with the first version focused on quests, inventory, the player sheet, local progress, and a simple medieval-inspired interface.

For updates, early interest, or questions, join the list on the homepage or follow the project below.

What Life Quest Is About

Life Quest is not a normal productivity app. It is a way to turn ordinary life into meaningful quests: cooking, training, building, repairing, organizing, exploring, learning, caring for home, managing resources, journaling, and becoming more capable.

The goal is not more screen time. The goal is more intentional living outside the app.
