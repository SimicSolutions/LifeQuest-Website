Life Quest Support

Help on the road.

You do not need to share private journal entries or health notes to ask for help.

Contact support

Email Simic Solutions Inc. at info@lifequestapp.ca for app issues, feedback, or feature requests. If you can, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version from Settings, and the steps that led to the issue.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

The current app keeps your quests, items, journals, and Player details on your device. It does not require a Life Quest account. Your Apple backup settings may affect whether that local data is included in a device backup.

Why does the map ask for my location?

Places can use location to show where you are and mark explored areas. You can deny access or change it in iOS Settings. The map is not required for your other Life Quest records.

How do I erase my Life Quest data?

In the app, open Settings and choose “Erase All Life Quest Data.” This permanently removes local Life Quest records from the app. If you are unsure, contact us before erasing anything.

How do I stop Life Quest emails?

If you signed up on the website, use the unsubscribe option in an email or write to info@lifequestapp.ca. App use does not require email signup.
